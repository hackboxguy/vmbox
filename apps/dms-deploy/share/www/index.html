To put the board into recovery mode:

  1. Power the board off.
  2. Connect the board's USB-C recovery port to this PC.
  3. Hold the FORCE RECOVERY button, tap RESET, then release RECOVERY.

The board will show up here within a few seconds. Nothing appears on its screen in recovery mode — that is normal.
